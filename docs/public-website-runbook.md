# Public website content runbook

## Publish or hide a product

Complete active variants and required attributes, activate the product, then edit it and enable **Publicly visible**. Enable **Show retail price online** only for an approved company retail price. Optionally enable **Feature on public website**. Drafting, archiving, or making the product private removes it from catalogue, search, direct lookup, sitemap, and anonymous image access.

Upload optimized JPEG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF images internally, choose a primary image, and provide meaningful alternative text. Keep descriptions factual and free of supplier terms, cost, margin, or internal notes.

## Branches and contact

Use **Branches** to opt an active branch into the public page after verifying its real contact details. Use **Public website** to manage hero copy, phone, email, and WhatsApp. Leave unavailable values empty rather than publishing placeholders. WhatsApp numbers should include the country code.

## Quotations

Authorized staff open **Quotations**, review contact and project information, then move requests through New, Contacted, In Progress, Closed, or Cancelled. Requests do not reserve stock or create sales automatically.
