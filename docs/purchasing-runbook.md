# Purchasing runbook

## Add a supplier

Open **Suppliers**, choose **Add supplier**, review existing names/contact details for obvious duplicates, enter the record, and save. Authorized management can edit or archive/reactivate it. Never delete historical suppliers.

## Create and order a purchase

Open **Purchases**, choose **New purchase**, select branch and supplier, add variant quantities and costs, then save. Review the database-calculated total and use **Mark ordered** when placed. Neither step changes stock.

## Receive delivery

Open an ordered purchase and choose **Receive goods**. Compare ordered, previously received, and remaining quantities. Enter only physically present goods and confirm. Inventory updates immediately and the GRN is immutable. Repeat for later partial deliveries.

## Record payment

Choose **Record payment** on a purchase. Confirm the displayed balance, enter an amount no greater than it, select the method, and confirm. Review purchase payment history and the supplier statement for outstanding balance.

## Exceptions

Cancel only a draft or ordered purchase with no receipt. For an incorrect GRN, do not edit the receipt, movement, or inventory balance. Preserve it, notify management, and document a future reversal. Never correct purchasing by directly editing inventory.
